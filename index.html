Medical Exam OCR

Convert question-paper photos and scanned PDFs into searchable, copyable text PDFs.

1. Choose your exam pages

For best results, use straight, sharp, well-lit images. English medical terminology usually works best with “English”; choose “English + Bangla” for mixed-language papers.

Ready. Your files are processed in this browser; they are not uploaded to a server by this app.

2. Review and correct the OCR text

OCR can confuse similar characters, question numbers, columns, tables, and symbols. Check every page against the original before exporting—especially answer options and medical notation.

Important accuracy note

This tool produces a text-based PDF from OCR; it does not guarantee a perfect facsimile of the original page design. Complex tables, two-column questions, diagrams, handwritten notes, subscripts/superscripts, Greek letters, and mathematical symbols may need manual correction. The PDF is selectable/searchable because it is rebuilt from recognized text, not because the original page image is embedded.
